    How it was made

    A story bible fixed the world, the rules of the house and its fourteen endings. An outline turned that into a graph: every scene, choice and flag, no prose yet. Four act writers then drafted their acts in parallel against the outline. A build step merged the acts and validated the graph, and a simulator walked it until every ending was proven reachable. A critic read for cohesion and voice; a playtest pass went through it as a player would. Everything is prebaked: there is no runtime AI and no backend. The house you visit is one static file of words.
